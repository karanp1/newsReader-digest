### Critical ban: On recycling of lithium-ion batteries
13 Nov 2026 | https://www.thehindu.com/opinion/editorial/critical-ban-on-recycling-of-lithium-ion-batteries/article71699389.ece

• India's Directorate General of Foreign Trade banned lithium-ion battery black mass exports on November 10, moving it from the free to the prohibited list.

• Black mass, the shredded intermediate from spent batteries and factory scrap, contains lithium, nickel, cobalt, manganese and graphite.
