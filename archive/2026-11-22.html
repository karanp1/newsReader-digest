What the company does
  • MTAR Technologies makes precision-engineered components such as ball screws, water-lubricated bearings and electro-mechanical actuation systems for defence, aerospace, nuclear and clean energy customers from seven units in Hyderabad.
  • It earns from supplying these parts to Indian government programmes and export customers, with a consolidated order book of ₹5,976.5 crore as on 30 September 2026, per its investor presentation.

Prime Focus Ltd (PFOCUS, -5.41%)

What the sector does
  • Media and entertainment services sell post-production work such as visual effects, digital intermediate and 2D-to-3D conversion to film studios, streaming platforms and broadcasters.
  • Revenue comes from project fees and long-term contracts, while costs are mostly skilled labour, software licences and studio infrastructure, so margins swing with utilisation and wage inflation.
What the company does
  • Prime Focus Ltd provides post-production and visual effects services from 18 cities across five continents, including India's largest integrated studio in Mumbai.
  • It earns from media and entertainment clients worldwide, with revenue of ₹1,429 crore in the September 2026 quarter and a market capitalisation of about ₹19,680 crore.
