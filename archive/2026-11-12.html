What the sector does
  • Fertiliser makers sell complex fertilisers such as DAP and NPK grades to farmers, mostly through subsidised retail channels, with the government fixing nutrient-based subsidy rates each season.

  • FACT is India's first large-scale fertiliser plant, set up in 1943 at Udyogamandal, Kochi, and is a central public sector enterprise under the Department of Fertilizers.
  • It makes complex fertiliser Factamfos, ammonium sulphate and caprolactam, and trades potash, NPK and organic manures, selling mainly to farmers in southern India.
Why it moved today

  • The subsidy covers DAP and NPKS grades, with DAP retail price unchanged at ₹1,350 per 50-kg bag, which supports fertiliser makers' working capital and realisations.
The numbers (Screener)
  • FACT's September 2025 quarter sales were ₹1,629 crore, up 12% year-on-year, with operating profit of ₹39 crore and a 2.4% operating margin.
  • The December 2025 quarter swung to a net loss of ₹68 crore on sales of ₹1,568 crore, while the stock trades at 38.3 times book value with ROCE of 8.65% and ROE of 1.61%.
What the numbers say
  • FACT's P/E of 3,553 and 38.3 times book value are far above peers like Chambal Fertilisers (P/E 8.19) and Coromandel International (P/E 36.80), reflecting weak and volatile earnings rather than a cheap valuation.
