Jio Platforms

  • Jio Platforms raised $3.2bn in India's largest-ever IPO and shares surged 21% on debut, valuing the company at $131.5bn.
  • The listing made Jio India's second most valuable company, behind parent Reliance Industries, with shares up 27.8% over the IPO price.
  • Jio serves more than 530 million customers, with Meta and Google among major shareholders after their 2020 investments.
  • Jio is weighing a low-Earth-orbit satellite internet service that would compete with Elon Musk's Starlink in India.
  • Jio's 2016 entry cut India's average telecom revenue per subscriber 42.8% to $0.75 by 2019, and seven of 10 incumbent operators exited.

Sources: aljazeera.com, tradingview.com

Outlook: Bullish

A $3.2bn IPO, a 21% debut pop and a $131.5bn valuation show strong demand for India's largest telecom operator. The 530 million subscriber base and big-tech backers support the growth case.

Bitcoin

  • J.P. Morgan's October 2026 report projects Bitcoin will return 10% annually over the next 10 years, versus 7.5% for large U.S. stocks.
  • The bank expects 55% annual volatility for Bitcoin against 18% for large U.S. stocks, giving both the same 0.32 points of return above cash per point of volatility.
  • Michael Saylor said the next bull market is already underway, citing Bitcoin's bounce off its 200-week moving average after the June 30 low of $58,525.
  • Profit-taking now outweighs loss-taking about four coins to one, a regime shift that has marked every bull market since 2011, with the next halving due in early April 2028.
  • James Lavish said early holders have sold and young investors moved to prediction markets and sports betting, though he called the bottom in with a base case of $400,000 to $500,000 in 2029.

Sources: finance.yahoo.com, fool.com, finance.yahoo.com, fool.com

Outlook: Neutral

J.P. Morgan's 10% return forecast beats stocks but carries 55% volatility, so risk-adjusted returns match. Saylor and Lavish see the bottom in, yet retail sentiment stays bearish and early holders have sold.

Gold

  • MCX December silver plunged ₹10,577 per kg to ₹1,79,600, its steepest one-day drop, while gold futures fell ₹4,347 per 10 grams to ₹1,38,249, the lowest since late March.
  • Spot gold dropped $145.84, or nearly 4%, to $3,875.55 per ounce on Friday, its lowest in over a year, and spot silver shed $3.04, or 6%, to $49.12 per ounce.
  • The US PCE price index rose 0.5% month-on-month in September with annual inflation at 3.6%, and the Fed held rates at 3.75%-4%, with traders pricing roughly a 75% probability of a December hike.
  • Silver has lost more than 50% from its January record high of ₹4,20,048 per kg, and gold is down ₹42,321 per 10 grams from its peak of ₹1,80,779.
  • India's festive and wedding purchases, with Dhanteras on November 6, 2026, plus steady central bank buying, are expected to support demand in November.

Sources: livemint.com, livemint.com, finance.biggo.com, finance.biggo.com

Outlook: Bearish

Hot US inflation at 3.6% and a 75% odds of a December Fed hike are lifting real yields and crushing non-yielding metals. Record ETF inflows and Indian festive demand cushion but do not reverse the rate-driven selloff.
