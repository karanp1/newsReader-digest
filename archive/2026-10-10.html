MAS consulted in November 2025 and kept its principles-based approach after respondents backed it. Implementation is phased: sections 3 and 4 by October 2027, sections 5 and 6 by October 2028. MAS plans to consult in 2027 on extra guidance for agentic AI, systems that act autonomously and reach for tools.
