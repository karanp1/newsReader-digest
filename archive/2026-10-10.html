EconomyWhat are the reforms proposed by the GST Council?

09 Oct 2026

The GST Council's 57th meeting approved procedural reforms, not rate changes, to take effect April 1, 2027. Registration moves to a blacklist model, where most applications are accepted automatically; 61% of taxpayers already get registration within three working days. Small e-commerce sellers will register only in their home state.
