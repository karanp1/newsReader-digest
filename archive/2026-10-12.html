El Nino sets temperature record in key Pacific zone: EU monitor

12 Oct 2026

Sea surface temperatures in the Nino3.4 zone of the Pacific hit an all-time high of 29.70C in September, the EU's Copernicus Climate Change Service said, beating the peaks of the strong El Ninos of 1982/83, 1997/98 and 2015/16. El Nino is the warming phase of a natural cycle that shifts winds and rainfall worldwide. Copernicus stopped short of calling this the strongest on record, since a new index that adjusts for warmer tropical oceans suggests past events still rank higher, and Japan's weather agency instead called it the strongest on record. Global sea surface temperatures also set a September record of 21.05C, and September air temperatures ran 1.63C above pre-industrial levels. Watch whether the event, still short of its peak, pushes 2027 to a new global record.
