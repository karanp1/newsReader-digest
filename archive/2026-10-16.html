MistralIntroducing Timestral

15 Oct 2026

  • Timestral is a new family of time-series foundation models whose largest version has 209M parameters and ranks first on GIFT-Eval, TIME and fev-bench.
  • The 209M model beats the previous state of the art using only two-thirds of its parameters, from a single checkpoint without ensembling.
  • The family spans 5M, 28M, 123M and 209M parameters, all on the Pareto front of accuracy versus model size, with the 28M model beating Chronos-2, about four times larger.
  • Timestral runs on a standard server CPU without a GPU, forecasting one day ahead from three weeks of 15-minute history in 10 ms with the 5M model and 140 ms with the 209M model.
  • It delivers probabilistic, multivariate forecasts with past-only and known-future covariates, so plants can model sensor dependencies and plan maintenance around uncertainty.
