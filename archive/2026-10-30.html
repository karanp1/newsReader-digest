Open to investigation: On the challenge to the Digital Personal Data Protection Act, 2023

29 Oct 2026

https://www.thehindu.com/opinion/editorial/open-to-investigation-on-the-challenge-to-the-digital-personal-data-protection-act-2023/article71641118.ece

  • The Digital News Publishers Association has challenged the Digital Personal Data Protection Act, 2023, and its Rules in the Supreme Court.
  • The Act lacks a journalistic exemption that appeared in the 2018, 2019 and 2021 drafts, so reporters could be treated as data fiduciaries.
  • Non-compliance can attract penalties of up to ₹250 crore, an existential threat to smaller and independent newsrooms.
  • The petition also challenges Section 44(3), which amended the RTI Act, 2005, removing the public-interest qualification for disclosing personal information.
  • The Supreme Court, while issuing notice, stressed balancing press freedom with privacy, and the Centre's willingness to meet editors is a welcome step toward consensus.
