The Hindu - Editorials

Pressure tactics: On the new U.S. tariffs on India

26 Nov 2026

https://www.thehindu.com/opinion/editorial/pressure-tactics-on-the-new-us-tariffs-on-india/article71747021.ece

  • The U.S. Trade Representative has proposed a 25% tariff on India over alleged strategic excess manufacturing capacity, the second-highest rate among 14 countries it has acted against.
  • The real pressure is not the 25% rate but the 17% it could fall to if India signs a trade deal with the U.S.
  • The 25% rate, net of existing tariffs, matches the 25% Trump imposed in July 2025 and is near the 26% announced in April 2025.
  • The 17% rate is barely below the 18% agreed in the February 2026 joint statement and one point above tariffs on Indonesia and Malaysia.
  • The editorial argues India lacks excess capacity, since manufacturing's share of GDP is lower than a decade ago, and should sign a deal only if benefits at least equal costs.

Indefensible defiance: On Tamil Nadu and Court’s Thirupparankundram order

26 Nov 2026

https://www.thehindu.com/opinion/editorial/indefensible-defiance-on-tamil-nadu-and-courts-thirupparankundram-order/article71747044.ece

  • Tamil Nadu defied a Supreme Court direction to light the Karthigai Deepam at a stone pillar on Thirupparankundram hills, lighting it only at the traditional spot on November 24.
  • The hills house the Arulmigu Subramaniya Swamy, Uchchi Pillaiyar and Kashi Viswanathar temples, a Sultan Sikkandar Avulia Dargah and Jain beds, with the lamp historically lit near the Pillaiyar temple.
  • In 1994-95 the Hindu Munnani demanded the lamp be lit at a pillar 50 metres from the Dargah; courts in 1862 and 1912 had disallowed lamp lighting near the Dargah.
  • The Madras High Court's Madurai Bench relied on a 1931 Privy Council ruling to hold the temple management obliged to light the lamp at the Deepathoon; the Supreme Court stayed this, allowing it this year only.
  • The Collector apologised to the Court, blaming the temple's Board of Trustees, leaving the State exposed to charges of colluding to breach a judicial mandate.
