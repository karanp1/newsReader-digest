### Piyush Goyal: H-1B curbs good for Indian economy
12 Oct 2026 | https://economictimes.indiatimes.com/tech/newsletters/morning-dispatch/the-et-startup-awards-2026-special/articleshow/134880370.cms

Commerce and industry minister Piyush Goyal said not getting more H-1B visas is good for the Indian economy. ### Groww, Meesho founders: list only when business is predictable
12 Oct 2026 | https://economictimes.indiatimes.com/tech/newsletters/morning-dispatch/the-et-startup-awards-2026-special/articleshow/134880370.cms

Startups should go public only when their businesses have achieved a degree of predictability, according to Meesho cofounder and CEO Vidit Aatrey and Groww cofounder and CEO Lalit Keshre. They spoke on a panel at the ET Startup Awards 2026 moderated by ETtech editor Samidha Sharma.

Meesho and Groww, both around a decade old, listed on Indian exchanges a month apart late last year. Aatrey said early-stage founders in India are increasingly looking at public markets as an alternative to raising private capital. In the US, small IPOs are rare, while in India both small and large listings are taking place, he said, adding that founders are now thinking about short-circuiting and going public instead of raising private capital.

Keshre said the question is more about value. Capital is available where capital is needed, but at a certain stage valuing an asset is very tough. In private markets, two parties are deciding; in public markets, there are a lot more parties, he said. Groww listed on the stock exchanges last November.

Watch whether more Indian founders follow the small-IPO route Aatrey described.
