Open Claw NewsletterBetter Leads, Fewer Sales

23 Oct 2026

  • A Chinese online health-insurance brokerage routed low-intent leads to AI voice agents and high-intent leads to human workers, studied across more than half a million customer outreach records.
  • High-intent leads concentrated early in the workday, raising the chance a call was high-intent by about 23 percentage points on the first three calls and fading to zero later.
  • Human customer acquisition fell about 5 percentage points on the first three calls, exactly where lead enrichment was greatest, and flattened over the day.
  • Workers reallocated their earliest leads about 8 percentage points more often and shortened those calls by about 13%, consistent with a higher reservation value for persisting.
  • A calibrated decomposition found the modest gain from better lead ordering was outweighed by the larger negative behavioral response, making opportunity distribution a key service operations variable.
