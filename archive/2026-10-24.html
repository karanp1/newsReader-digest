Open Claw NewsletterBetter Leads, Fewer Sales

23 Oct 2026

  • A Chinese online health-insurance brokerage routed low-intent leads to AI voice agents and high-intent leads to human workers, studied across more than half a million customer outreach records.
  • High-intent leads concentrated early in the workday, raising the chance a call was high-intent by about 23 percentage points on the first three calls and fading to zero later.
  • Human customer acquisition fell about 5 percentage points on the first three calls, exactly where lead enrichment was greatest, and flattened over the day.
  • Workers reallocated their earliest leads about 8 percentage points more often and shortened those calls by about 13%, consistent with a higher reservation value for persisting.
  • A calibrated decomposition found the modest gain from better lead ordering was outweighed by the larger negative behavioral response, making opportunity distribution a key service operations variable.

What the sector does
  • India's food delivery and quick commerce sector sells restaurant meals and groceries delivered in minutes to urban consumers through apps.
  • Revenue comes from order commissions, delivery fees, advertising and subscription charges, while costs are delivery rider pay, dark store rents and discounts.
What the company does
  • Swiggy runs a convenience app offering food delivery from restaurants, quick commerce groceries via Instamart, and out-of-home dining.
  • It earns from order commissions, delivery and platform fees, and advertising, with food delivery its main cash generator.
Why it moved today
  • Swiggy shares fell 16.75% to ₹176 after Q2 FY27 results disclosed Instamart's adjusted EBITDA loss widened to ₹895 crore from ₹778 crore in the previous quarter and ₹849 crore a year earlier.
  • Multiple brokerages cut target prices, including Nomura to ₹300 from ₹435 and JM Financial to ₹200 from ₹255, citing Instamart's worsening contribution margin of negative 1.4% from negative 0.3%.
The numbers (Screener)
  • Market cap is ₹48,582 crore at a price of ₹176, with book value of ₹61.5, so the stock trades at 2.86 times book.
  • ROCE is -24.1% and ROE is -29.1%; September 2026 quarter sales were ₹7,571 crore with a net loss of ₹922 crore and EPS of -3.34.
What the numbers say
  • Swiggy trades at 2.86 times book against a peer median P/E of 49.39, but it is loss-making with negative ROCE of -24.1%, unlike Eternal which has positive ROCE of 2.48% and a net profit of ₹125 crore.
  • Sales grew 11% sequentially to ₹7,571 crore, yet the net loss narrowed only 15.6% year-on-year as Instamart's losses offset food delivery's ₹341 crore adjusted EBITDA.

Jammu Kashmir Bank Ltd (J&KBANK, -16.28%)

What the sector does
  • Indian banks take deposits from households and firms and lend to retail, farm, MSME and corporate borrowers, earning the spread between lending rates and deposit costs.
  • Revenue is driven by loan growth and net interest margin, while cost is set by deposit rates and operating expenses; large private players include HDFC Bank, ICICI Bank, Kotak Mahindra Bank and Axis Bank.
What the company does
  • Jammu & Kashmir Bank is a private-sector bank offering retail, corporate and treasury banking, and is the only private bank convening State/UT Level Bankers' Committee meetings.
  • It holds lead bank responsibility in 12 districts of Jammu & Kashmir, giving it a strong regional deposit franchise and government salary-account relationships.
Why it moved today
  • The stock fell 16.28% to ₹113.09 on 23 October 2026, after management cut its FY27 CASA ratio target to 42%+ from 45% and dropped its 3.5% margin guidance on the earnings call.
  • The bank reported Q2FY27 net profit of ₹546.36 crore, up 10.6% year on year, but treasury income of ₹92 crore drove the gain while net interest income rose just 5%.
The numbers (Screener)
  • The stock trades at a P/E of 5.27 and 0.71 times book value of ₹159, with a market cap of ₹12,453 crore and dividend yield of 0.00%.
  • Net profit was ₹581 crore in Dec 2025, ₹799 crore in Mar 2026, ₹429 crore in Jun 2026 and ₹553 crore in Sep 2026, with EPS of ₹5.28, ₹7.25, ₹3.89 and ₹5.02.
What the numbers say
  • At a P/E of 5.27, J&K Bank is the cheapest in its peer table, where HDFC Bank trades at 13.34, ICICI Bank at 16.95 and Kotak Mahindra Bank at 21.38.
  • Its ROE of 15.4% and ROCE of 5.84% sit below peers like ICICI Bank and Axis Bank, while the 16% fall reflects concerns over margin pressure and reliance on one-time treasury gains.
