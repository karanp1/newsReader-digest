  • The Supreme Court has again pulled up the Rajasthan government over industrial effluents flowing into the Jojari river, endangering people who depend on it.
  • The Jojari, Luni and Bandi are seasonal rivers in arid western Rajasthan, flowing through Jodhpur, Balotra and Pali districts.
  • Untreated or insufficiently treated effluents from textile-dyeing and steelworking factories threaten the health of around 20 lakh local residents.
