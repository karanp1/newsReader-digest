### AI will fuel another wave of startup creation in two years: AWS ventures chief
09 Oct 2026 | https://economictimes.indiatimes.com/tech/newsletters/morning-dispatch/winners-of-the-et-startup-awards-2026-to-be-feted-today/articleshow/134804055.cms

Artificial intelligence is lowering barriers to building companies and is likely to fuel another wave of startup creation over the next two years, as founders build products faster and operate with smaller teams, according to Jason Bennett, vice president and global head of startups and venture capital at Amazon Web Services.

"We have more startups being created today than there were two years ago. And I suspect if you look out two years from now, you'll see a further increase," Bennett said. AI-native startups are showing faster revenue growth and gaining market traction earlier than traditional startups. An AWS study with Strand Partners found AI-native startups growing revenues 167% year-on-year and are 5.5 times more likely to reach $1 million in earnings than non-AI-native peers. "Some companies are getting to hundreds of millions in Annual Recurring Revenue (ARR) very quickly," Bennett said.

AWS is increasingly supporting startups beyond cloud infrastructure, helping with go-to-market and enterprise customer acquisition through AWS Marketplace. "It's not enough for us just to provide credits or to have a platform of services… We also have to help them drive engagement and win customers," Bennett said. Watch whether the predicted wave shows up in funding and formation data.

This week, 28 Indian startups raised $113.35 million across 3 growth-stage deals, 22 early-stage deals, and 3 undisclosed rounds. The week also saw 8 key appointments, 3 fund launches and 2 mergers & acquisitions.

In contrast, 24 startups had collectively secured about $411.46 million in the previous week.

Growth-stage startups raised $55.3 million across three deals this week, led by lifestyle-tech brand DailyObjects, which secured Rs 332 crore (nearly $35 million) in a Series C round led by Xponentia Capital Partners, Anicut Capital and Axiom Asia Private Capital.

Smart kitchen appliance startup Beyond Appliances raised Rs 110 crore ($11.4 million) in a Series B round led by Fireside Ventures, while MSME-focused digital lender NeoGrowth secured Rs 85 crore in equity and debt funding from FMO and LeapFrog Investments.

[ Early-stage deals ]

Early-stage startups raised $58.05 million across 22 deals this week, led by photonic quantum technology startup Quanfluence, which secured $10 million from Chiratae Ventures and Rainmatter, followed by edtech startup byteXL with $9 million from Elevar Equity.

Medtech startup Sunfox Technologies raised $7 million in a Series A round co-led by Ashish Kacholia, Lashit Sanghvi, Alchemy Capital and 3one4 Capital, while quantum computing startup QpiAI secured Rs 50 crore in debt funding from InnoVen Capital.

Other funded startups included Omnichannel menswear brand Kisah, Wealth management startup Zomint, AI startup Desible.ai , healthtech SuperMush, Cold-pressed oil brand Gramiyaa, among others.

Travel tech platform TravClan, financial information platform MoneyTruVa, and Biofortified staples brand Better Nutrition also raised funding this week but did not disclose the amounts.

[ City and segment-wise deals ]

Bengaluru again led the funding charts with 10 startup deals during the week, followed by Delhi-NCR and Mumbai with 6 deals and 3 deals respectively. Hyderabad, Dehradun, Chandigarh and others also recorded deals.

E-commerce startups led the sectors with 8 deals, followed by AI and healthtech startups which grabbed 5 deals and 4 deals respectively. Meanwhile fintech, foodtech, SaaS, edtech, deeptech and others also saw funding activity.

[ Series-wise deals ]

Seed rounds led the week with 13 deals, followed by pre-seed and Series A startups which grabbed 5 deals each. Series C, Series B, debt and pre-Series A recorded a deal each.

[ Week-on-week funding trend ]

On a weekly basis, startup funding dropped over 72% to $113.35 million from $411.46 million raised in the previous week.

The average weekly funding over the past eight weeks stood at around $231.6 million across 22 deals.

Eight key appointments were announced this week. Sorry Sugar named Palash Arneja as Founder and CEO, while Google Cloud appointed Amit Kumar as interim India head. Snabbit appointed Ramesh Srinivasan as Head of Growth and Revenue, ALTEN India named Prabhakar Atla as CEO, and Eggoz onboarded actor Boman Irani as its brand ambassador.

  Singapore's financial regulator will start enforcing AI risk rules on 7 October 2027, a full year after publishing them. The Monetary Authority of Singapore's guidelines apply to every financial institution and every form of AI, but firms can scale their controls to the risk.
