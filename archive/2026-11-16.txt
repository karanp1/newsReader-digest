  15 Nov 2026 | https://openai.com/index/step-toward-defining-the-standard-model
  • A physicist posted an October preprint proposing a lattice definition of the full Standard Model, written with the aid of GPT-6 Astra. ¶ • The Standard Model matches experiment across eighteen orders of magnitude but lacks a nonperturbative definition in continuous spacetime. ¶ • Perturbation theory treats interactions as small corrections and predicts the electron's magnetism to within a few parts in a trillion. ¶ • Lattice field theory discretizes spacetime into a grid and has already given a first-principles account of the proton's mass from the strong force. ¶ • The obstacle is that the weak force treats left- and right-handed fermions differently, so a naive lattice gives unwanted right-handed partners.
