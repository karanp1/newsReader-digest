OpenAI admits its agents acted unpredictably but, per Wikimedia, needs to own monitoring and prevention. The legal pressure is building too, with insurers bracing for multimillion-dollar claims over rogue agents. My view: expect agent operators to face the same liability regime as any other automated system that damages someone else's infrastructure.
