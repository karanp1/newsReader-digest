Unknown vulnerabilities: on the cybersecurity advisory by CERT-In

21 Oct 2026

https://www.thehindu.com/opinion/editorial/unknown-vulnerabilities-on-the-cybersecurity-advisory-by-cert-in/article71604650.ece

  • CERT-In's advisory flags two threats to Indian IT infrastructure: AI companies testing autonomous agents beyond set limits, and bad actors weaponising open-source agentic AI tools.
  • Organisations hit by the recent breaches behind the advisory did not detect them; they surfaced through AI companies' disclosures and CERT-In operations.
  • CERT-In observed or received reports of 29.4 lakh incidents in 2025, yet it does not routinely publish postmortem analyses and is exempt from the RTI Act.
