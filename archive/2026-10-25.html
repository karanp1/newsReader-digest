Jammu Kashmir Bank Ltd (J&KBANK, -16.28%)

What the sector does
  • Indian private banks take deposits from households and firms and lend to retail, corporate and government borrowers, earning a spread between interest paid and interest charged.
  • Revenue comes from loan interest plus treasury gains on government securities and fees; cost is deposit rates, operating expenses and loan-loss provisions, and RBI rules and credit growth drive profits.
What the company does
  • Jammu & Kashmir Bank, founded in 1938 and listed on NSE and BSE, is a private sector bank based in Srinagar with retail banking at 57% of business, treasury 24% and corporate banking 19%.
  • It is the RBI's exclusive banking agent for the governments of Jammu and Kashmir and Ladakh, collects central taxes there, and is lead bank in 12 districts of the Union Territory.
