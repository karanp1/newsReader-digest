### Wicked surprise: On the northeast monsoon
25 Oct 2026 | https://www.thehindu.com/opinion/editorial/wicked-surprise-on-the-northeast-monsoon/article71623526.ece

• India's June-September southwest monsoon rainfall was 759.4 mm against 868.6 mm, a 12.6% deficit and the driest in 11 years.

• The India Meteorological Department expects northeast monsoon rainfall from October to December to be normal, within a 12% error window.

• Most of India is expected to get below-normal rain, except parts of northwest India and southern peninsular India, where it may be normal to above-normal.

• The northeast monsoon's 12% error window is wider than the 5% margin for southwest monsoon forecasts, so it can bring nasty surprises.

• The article urges the Centre and southern States to clear stormwater drains, fortify shelters, prepare for infrastructure damage and tighten weather warnings.
