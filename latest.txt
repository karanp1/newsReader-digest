### ETSA champions show the great Indian dream is all home-brewed and visa-free
12 Oct 2026 | https://economictimes.indiatimes.com/tech/newsletters/morning-dispatch/the-et-startup-awards-2026-special/articleshow/134880370.cms

• Commerce and industry minister Piyush Goyal discussed India's trade deals, US relations, foreign investment, and benefits from green card restrictions with ET's Deepshikha Sikarwar.

• On H-1B curbs, Goyal said not getting more visas is good for the Indian economy as top talent that went to the US could have built businesses in India.

• On India-US relations, he said intrinsic engagement will always have friction points but both countries recognize the relationship is very consequential.

• On FDI outflows, he attributed stock market outflows to the greater attraction of Western tech stocks and a narrower interest rate differential between US and Indian bonds.

### List when the business is predictable, and stay transparent after: Groww, Meesho founders
12 Oct 2026 | https://economictimes.indiatimes.com/tech/newsletters/morning-dispatch/the-et-startup-awards-2026-special/articleshow/134880370.cms

• Meesho cofounder and CEO Vidit Aatrey and Groww cofounder and CEO Lalit Keshre said startups should go public only when their businesses have achieved predictability.

• Meesho and Groww, both around a decade old, listed on Indian exchanges a month apart late last year but differ on why some startups seek an early IPO.

• Aatrey said early-stage founders increasingly see public markets as an alternative to private capital, noting small IPOs are rare in the US but common in India.

• Keshre said the question is about value, as valuing an asset is tough in private markets where two parties decide, but public markets have many more parties.

### ET Startup Awards 2026: Deeptech wants government to step up as the first customer
