### Global pick: Hugging Face’s new AI model lets you chat with DNA
Wired | https://www.wired.com/story/hugging-face-new-ai-biology-model-chat-with-dna/

• Hugging Face released Carbon-X, an open DNA model that runs locally on a laptop, built with Zhongguancun Academy and the University of Naples Federico II.

• Carbon-X combines DNA and natural language so users can ask about how DNA changes affect gene activity and health and get conversational answers.

• The team trained on 300 billion tokens of DNA sequences and English text, expanding the vocabulary to read DNA in groups of six letters.

• The model has 14 billion parameters and handles sequences of roughly 50,000 base pairs, but its predictions are not yet validated in wet lab experiments.

### Global pick: Big Tech’s thirst for water is sparking a global resistance
Rest of World | https://restofworld.org/2026/ai-datacenters-water-resistance-global/

• A 2023 University of California paper estimated training the large language model behind the first ChatGPT used 185,000 gallons of water.

• A hyperscale data center can consume 1 to 5 million gallons of water a day, comparable to a city of 10,000 to 50,000 people.

• Google's proposed $200 million Cerrillos data center was projected to use 169 liters of water per second, as much daily as all 80,000 residents use for drinking in a year.

• After residents voted 49% to 38% against the project and claims were filed in 2020, Google returned in February 2022 offering to cool the data center with air-conditioning instead of water.
