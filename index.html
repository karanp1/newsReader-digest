Open to investigation: On the challenge to the Digital Personal Data Protection Act, 2023

30 Oct 2026

https://www.thehindu.com/opinion/editorial/open-to-investigation-on-the-challenge-to-the-digital-personal-data-protection-act-2023/article71641118.ece

  • The Digital News Publishers Association has challenged the Digital Personal Data Protection Act, 2023, and its Rules in the Supreme Court.

Vedanta Power Ltd (VEDPOWER, +14.72%)

What the sector does
  • India's power generation sector sells electricity to state distribution utilities and industrial consumers under long-term power purchase agreements and merchant sales.
  • Revenue depends on units sold and tariff per kilowatt-hour, while cost is driven by coal prices, plant availability and interest on the heavy debt used to build plants.
What the company does
  • Vedanta Power Ltd is the power generation arm of the Vedanta Group, operating thermal plants and supplying electricity to industries and state utilities across India.
  • Its assets include the Jharsuguda plant in Odisha, Talwandi Sabo in Punjab, Meenakshi Energy in Andhra Pradesh and the Sakti plant in Chhattisgarh, with a 1,200 MW project under commissioning.
Why it moved today
  • Vedanta Power shares jumped after its September quarter results showed a net profit of ₹83 crore, a turnaround from a ₹1,688 crore loss a year earlier, with revenue up 38% to ₹2,908 crore.
  • The stock had fallen to a record low of ₹27.50 earlier in the week, and the results, including EBITDA up 122% to ₹421 crore, drove the rebound.
The numbers (Screener)
  • Stock P/E is 35.4 on a market cap of ₹12,955 crore and a current price of ₹33.1, against a 52-week high of ₹52.5 and low of ₹27.5.
  • September 2026 quarter sales were ₹2,908 crore with operating profit of ₹421 crore (OPM 14%) and net profit of ₹83 crore (EPS ₹0.21), versus a net loss of ₹1,689 crore in the September 2025 quarter.
What the numbers say
  • At a P/E of 35.4, Vedanta Power trades above NTPC's 11.05 and NHPC's 20.57 but below Adani Green's 109.49 and JSW Energy's 38.91, even though its quarterly net profit of ₹83 crore is far smaller than NTPC's ₹6,896 crore.
  • The September 2026 quarter net profit of ₹83 crore follows losses of ₹1,689 crore and ₹423 crore in the prior two reported quarters, so the earnings base is thin and volatile, and the 14.72% move reflects a recovery from a record low rather than a proven earnings trend.

Vedanta Oil And Gas Ltd (VOGL, +8.51%)

What the sector does
  • Oil and gas exploration and production companies find and pump crude oil and natural gas, selling to refiners, gas marketers and industrial buyers.
  • Revenue tracks global crude and gas prices and production volumes, while costs are lifting, processing and royalties; ONGC and Oil India dominate India's upstream.
What the company does
  • Vedanta Oil and Gas, which operates under the Cairn brand, is a private-sector explorer with interests in 44 blocks covering over 47,000 sq km across India.
  • It produces from Rajasthan, Andhra Pradesh, Gujarat and Assam, with gross 2P and 2C resources of 1.4 billion barrels of oil equivalent, and earned ₹2,488 crore revenue in the September 2026 quarter.
Why it moved today
  • The stock jumped after the September-quarter results, with net profit of ₹746 crore against a ₹91 crore loss a year earlier and EBITDA up 34% to ₹1,393 crore, per the Economic Times.
  • The shares had fallen to a record low of ₹26.41 the previous week, and the results lifted the price back above ₹30 for the first time in nearly two weeks.
The numbers (Screener)
  • Stock P/E is 20.8 and price to book is 0.64 times, with book value at ₹47.0 per share and a market cap of ₹11,821 crore.
  • September 2026 quarter sales were ₹2,488 crore, operating margin 54%, net profit ₹746 crore and EPS ₹1.91, against a ₹479 crore loss in the March 2026 quarter.
What the numbers say
  • At 20.8 times earnings the stock trades in line with the peer median P/E of 20.77, but it is far more expensive than ONGC at 6.49 and Oil India at 8.84.
  • The 54% operating margin in the September quarter is the highest in the data shown, yet ROE is negative 1.22% and ROCE is not reported, so returns on capital remain weak.

Sources: m.economictimes.com, cnbctv18.com, themachinemaker.com

Intellect Design Arena Ltd (INTELLECT, -16.31%)

What the sector does
  • Indian IT services firms sell software development, maintenance and platform licences to banks, insurers and other enterprises worldwide, billing mostly in dollars and euros.
  • Revenue comes from licence fees, annual maintenance contracts and per-hour services, while cost is dominated by salaries, so margins swing with wage hikes, utilisation and currency.
What the company does
  • Intellect Design Arena, incorporated in 2011 and based in Chennai, sells banking, insurance, risk and treasury software products to financial institutions globally.
  • It earns from platform subscriptions, licences and annual maintenance contracts; licence-linked revenue was ₹457 crore in the September 2026 quarter, with 21 new customer wins.
Why it moved today
  • Shares fell about 17% on October 30 after September-quarter net profit rose just 2% year on year to ₹104 crore and EBITDA margin slipped to 19.8% from 20%.
  • Context, not the cause: revenue rose 11.7% year on year to ₹846.7 crore and was flat sequentially, and the stock had already fallen nearly 48% in 2026 before this session.
The numbers (Screener)

What the numbers say

  • IT and mobility distribution buys hardware, smartphones and cloud services from global brands and resells them to retailers, resellers and enterprises across India, the Middle East and Africa.

What the company does
  • Redington distributes IT products, smartphones, consumer electronics and cloud services for over 300 global technology brands across more than 40 markets in India, the Middle East, Africa, South Asia and Turkey.
  • It earns a distribution margin on goods sold plus supply chain and support services fees, with September 2026 quarter sales of ₹34,922 crore and net profit of ₹453 crore.
Why it moved today
  • The news does not explain the 8.46% fall; the only article is a 30 October 2026 exchange notice scheduling the Q2FY27 earnings call for 4 November 2026, which contains no financial results.
  • Context only: the June 2026 quarter showed sales of ₹34,922 crore, up 34.57% year on year, and net profit of ₹453 crore, up 76.55%, with the results call due four days after this digest.
The numbers (Screener)
  • Stock P/E is 14.2, price to book is about 2.5 times (price ₹327 against book value ₹130), dividend yield 1.84%, ROCE 18.4% and ROE 16.9%.
  • Sales rose from ₹29,076 crore in September 2025 to ₹34,922 crore in June 2026, while net profit moved from ₹350 crore to ₹453 crore and operating margin stayed near 2.0%.
What the numbers say
  • At a P/E of 14.2, Redington trades below the peer median of 23.32 and below Creative Newtech at 20.71, while its ROCE of 18.4% beats the peer median of 6.25%.
  • Sales grew 34.57% and net profit 76.55% year on year in the June 2026 quarter, so the 8.46% fall looks like a sharp pullback in a stock that has run from a low of ₹191 to ₹327.
