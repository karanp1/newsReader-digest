Fouled waters: On Rajasthan’s river pollution

27 Oct 2026

https://www.thehindu.com/opinion/editorial/fouled-waters-on-rajasthans-river-pollution/article71631783.ece

  • The Supreme Court has again pulled up the Rajasthan government over industrial effluents flowing into the Jojari river, endangering people who depend on it.
  • The Jojari, Luni and Bandi are seasonal rivers in arid western Rajasthan, flowing through Jodhpur, Balotra and Pali districts.
  • Untreated or insufficiently treated effluents from textile-dyeing and steelworking factories threaten the health of around 20 lakh local residents.
  • The State told the Court in 2025 that sewage treatment plants handle only about half the sewage generated in the three urban centres, and the Court recorded substantial underutilisation at common effluent treatment plants.
  • The Court banned construction within 100 metres of the rivers' edges and hazardous industries within 500 m, and Justice Mehta said the State could be held liable under the 'polluter pays' principle.
