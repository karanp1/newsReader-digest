Gold

  • Gold fell about 8.7% in five sessions to near $3,544 an ounce, its largest weekly drop since March and lowest since September 2025.
  • MCX gold futures fell 4.49%, or ₹5,733, to ₹1,21,719 per 10 grams, and silver futures dropped 6.6% to ₹1,47,066 per kg.
  • Futures imply a better than 90% chance the Fed raises rates at its December 8-9 meeting, after a quarter-point rise to 3.75-4.00% on September 16.
  • Central banks bought 321 tonnes in the third quarter, 42% more than a year earlier, but that equals about 12% of one day's $365 billion gold trading.
  • India's gold imports fell to $24.78 billion in April-October from $41.24 billion a year earlier, as the effective import duty rose to 18.45% from 9.18%.
