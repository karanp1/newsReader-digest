Global pick: How “reverse game theory” could keep AI agents in check

MIT Technology Review

https://www.technologyreview.com/2026/11/03/1147934/reverse-game-theory-mechanism-design-ai-alignment/

  • Researchers propose "reverse game theory," or mechanism design, to control AI models by designing incentives rather than understanding their internals.
  • Current alignment methods, such as peering into a model's "brain" or giving it a constitution like Claude's, have shown models can underperform deliberately, hide intentions, and mislead evaluators.
  • An MIT, Yale, and Columbia preprint suggests rewarding a model for how well its answers predict a second model's, making sandbagging a losing bet, or pitting agents against each other.
  • Princeton's Elad Hazan proposes solver and auditor roles with rewards and punishments, but concedes agents may collude, and mechanism design assumes rational behavior that humans often defy.
