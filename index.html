OpenAICombatting adversarial distillation

20 Nov 2026

  • OpenAI says adversarial distillation lets bad actors train a second model on a frontier model's outputs, then deploy it without the original's safeguards.
  • It focuses on protecting chain-of-thought, the intermediate reasoning steps a model generates internally, which can carry information the final answer omits.
  • In an internal adaptive evaluation, the average attack success rate at recovering protected reasoning fell from 54.0% against GPT-6 Astra to 0.2% against GPT-6.2 Astra with updated safeguards.
  • In a cyber distillation study, a student trained on exact reasoning traces generally beat one trained on reasoning summaries after supervised fine-tuning, and still scored higher after reinforcement learning at the largest inference budget tested.
  • Defenses sit at two levels: request-level model behavior, classifiers and API controls, plus campaign-level detection of coordinated extraction attempts, with findings fed back into training and testing.
