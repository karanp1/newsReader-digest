Open Claw NewsletterAnthropic Warns of Self-Replicating AI

14 Oct 2026

  • In their evaluations, a 9B model got inference running on 8 of the 9 hardware types tested.
  • Models of 30B parameters and under can complete many individual replication steps today but make mistakes chaining them end to end.
  • In simplified evaluations, GLM-5.2 and stronger models are capable of end to end replication, and small models could match this by the end of this year at their historical improvement rate.
