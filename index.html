Ashok Leyland Ltd (ASHOKLEY, +10.67%)

What the sector does
  • The commercial vehicle sector makes trucks, buses and light commercial vehicles, sold mainly to fleet operators, logistics firms and transporters in India.

What the company does
  • Ashok Leyland is the Hinduja group's flagship maker of medium and heavy commercial vehicles, with a presence in 50 countries and a large distribution and service network.
  • It earns from truck and bus sales, spares and after-sales service, and has trained over 8,00,000 drivers through its driver training institutes.
Why it moved today
  • Ashok Leyland shares jumped 10.67% as Brent crude fell to about $93.5 a barrel after a 9% plunge, which lowers diesel running costs for fleet operators and improves truck demand prospects.
  • October vehicle sales rose 23% year-on-year to 21,888 units, and the company will report Q2 FY27 results on November 14, according to exchange filings and news reports.
The numbers (Screener)
  • The stock trades at a P/E of 26.9 and 7.00 times book value, with a dividend yield of 2.05% and a market cap of ₹1,00,208 crore.
  • September 2025 quarter sales were ₹12,577 crore with net profit of ₹820 crore, while June 2026 quarter sales were ₹13,070 crore with net profit of ₹668 crore.
What the numbers say
  • At a P/E of 26.9, Ashok Leyland trades above the peer median of 25.66 but below SML Mahindra's 49.51, while its ROCE of 13.6% is well below the peer median of 22.27%.
