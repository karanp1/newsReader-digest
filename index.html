Jio Platforms

  • Jio Platforms reported gross revenue of ₹47,485 crore for the September quarter, up 11.3% year-on-year and 3.3% sequentially.
  • EBITDA margin improved to 54.1% from 53.3% in the previous quarter, reflecting better operating performance.
  • Jio added 8.7 million subscribers in the quarter, taking its base past 542 million, with 35.6 million added year-on-year.
  • ARPU rose 1.5% sequentially and 3.5% year-on-year on a favourable subscriber mix and positive seasonality.
  • The stock surged 27% on its October 28 debut to ₹1,417.50 on the NSE, after a ₹30,200 crore IPO, the largest in India.

Sources: cnbctv18.com

Outlook: Bullish

Revenue up 11.3% year-on-year, EBITDA margin at 54.1% and 8.7 million net subscriber adds show steady operating momentum after listing. The 27% debut pop above the Rs 1,373 consensus target leaves valuation as the main risk.

Bitcoin

  • U.S. spot Bitcoin ETFs shed $643.2 million on Friday, their biggest single-day outflow of October, in the first full session after the $6.4 billion Binance hack.
  • Bitcoin fell about 4% Friday to roughly $76,400 before steadying near $77,520 Saturday, down 3.3% for the week.
  • Binance has covered $6 billion of the loss, restored withdrawals in phases from November 1, and partners froze about $3 billion, roughly half the haul.
  • Ethereum ETFs lost $155.7 million Friday, extending a five-day outflow streak to about $431 million, while Solana ETFs shed $10.8 million for a fifth straight down day.
  • The FBI attributed the attack to North Korea, and traders priced above 70% odds of a Fed rate hike in December after the Fed held rates at 3.75% to 4%.

Sources: decrypt.co, finance.yahoo.com

Outlook: Bearish

ETF outflows of $643.2 million, a 4% price drop and December rate-hike odds above 70% keep pressure on Bitcoin. Binance's $6 billion cover and $3 billion frozen limit the damage, but flows and macro lean negative.

Gold

  • Gold fell 3.29% to USD 3,898.90 an ounce on Friday, its lowest level since October 2025, and ended its worst October since 2008 at $3,875.
  • Hedge funds cut bullish gold bets for nine straight weeks, and investors added about 150 tonnes to gold ETFs since July, much of it now below cost, Saxo Bank said.
  • Gold broke below $4,000 and its 200-day moving average, which has turned into resistance, after disappointing Big Tech earnings and a tech-led equity selloff.
  • The Federal Reserve held interest rates at 3.75% to 4.00% at its October 27-28 meeting, keeping the opportunity cost of holding gold elevated.
  • US midterm elections on November 3 and Friday's October nonfarm payrolls report are the next catalysts, with a recovery above $4,000 seen calming the short-term picture.

Sources: m.economictimes.com, finance.yahoo.com, moneycontrol.com, fxleaders.com

Outlook: Bearish

Gold broke below $4,000 and its 200-day moving average, with hedge funds cutting bullish bets for nine straight weeks and ETF buyers underwater. Higher-for-longer US rates and firm oil keep pressure on; only weaker jobs data or a $4,000 reclaim would shift this.
