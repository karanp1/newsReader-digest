HAL

  • HAL is likely to be IAI's Indian partner for conversion work including local systems integration, wiring and structural updates, with at least 30% of the work in India.

Bitcoin

  • US spot Bitcoin ETFs lost $643.2 million on Friday, the first full session after the $6.4 billion Binance hack, their biggest single-day outflow of October.
  • Bitcoin fell about 4% to roughly $76,400 Friday on the hack, then steadied near $78,076 by Monday as Binance restored withdrawals and replenished about 95% of affected assets.
  • Spot Bitcoin ETFs ended October with about $516 million in net outflows, and total net assets fell to $98.1 billion from $111 billion on October 5.
  • Ethereum ETFs lost $155.7 million Friday, extending a five-day outflow streak to about $431 million and October losses to roughly $1.61 billion.
  • Strategy's Michael Saylor said the next Bitcoin bull market has started, citing the June bounce off the 200-week moving average and profits now outpacing losses by 4 to 1.

Sources: decrypt.co, finance.yahoo.com, au.finance.yahoo.com, 247wallst.com

Outlook: Bearish

ETF outflows of $643.2 million and October's $516 million net redemptions show institutions cutting exposure after the Binance hack, limiting Bitcoin's rebound near $78,000. A clean Binance reserve report on November 3 or a shift to inflows would decide the next move.

Gold

  • Gold fell 3.29% to USD 3,898.90 an ounce on Friday, its lowest level since October 2025, and ended its worst October since 2008 at $3,875.
  • Hedge funds cut bullish gold bets for nine straight weeks, and investors added about 150 tonnes to gold ETFs since July, much of it now below cost, Saxo Bank said.
  • Gold broke below $4,000 and its 200-day moving average, which has turned into resistance, after disappointing Big Tech earnings and a tech-led equity selloff.
  • The Federal Reserve held interest rates at 3.75% to 4.00% at its October 27-28 meeting, keeping the opportunity cost of holding gold elevated.
  • US midterm elections on November 3 and Friday's October nonfarm payrolls report are the next catalysts, with a recovery above $4,000 seen calming the short-term picture.

Sources: m.economictimes.com, finance.yahoo.com, moneycontrol.com, fxleaders.com

Outlook: Bearish

Gold broke below $4,000 and its 200-day moving average, with hedge funds cutting bullish bets for nine straight weeks and ETF buyers underwater. Higher-for-longer US rates and firm oil keep pressure on; only weaker jobs data or a $4,000 reclaim would shift this.

Vedanta Power Ltd (VEDPOWER, +14.72%)

What the sector does
  • Thermal power producers burn coal to generate electricity and sell it to state distribution utilities and industrial buyers under long-term power purchase agreements.
  • Revenue depends on units sold and tariffs, while coal cost, plant load factor and interest on project debt drive profitability; NTPC, Adani Power, Tata Power and JSW Energy are the large listed players.
What the company does
  • Vedanta Power runs 4,780 MW of thermal capacity across Punjab, Chhattisgarh, Andhra Pradesh and Odisha, including the 1,980 MW Talwandi Sabo plant and the 1,200 MW Sakti plant.
