Bitcoin

  • J.P. Morgan's October 2026 report projects Bitcoin will return 10% annually over 10 years, above its 7.5% forecast for large U.S. stocks.
  • J.P. Morgan expects 55% annual volatility for Bitcoin versus 18% for large U.S. stocks, giving both the same 0.32 return per unit of volatility.
  • Michael Saylor said the next Bitcoin bull market has started, citing the June 30 low of $58,525 and bank lending against Bitcoin.
  • Profit-taking now outnumbers loss-taking by about 4 to 1, flipping from 12 to 1 loss-taking at the June lows, a bull-market signal since 2011.
  • James Lavish said early holders have sold and younger investors moved to prediction markets and sports betting, but he still sees a bottom and a $400,000 to $500,000 top in 2029.

Sources: finance.yahoo.com, fool.com, finance.yahoo.com, finance.yahoo.com

Outlook: Neutral

J.P. Morgan sees 10% annual returns but equal volatility-adjusted returns to stocks, while Saylor and Lavish call a bottom. The split is between modest institutional forecasts and on-chain bull signals.

Gold

  • Spot gold plunged $145.84, or nearly 4%, to $3,875.55 per ounce on Friday, its lowest level in over a year.
  • MCX gold futures fell ₹4,347 to ₹1,38,419 per 10 grams, while silver dropped ₹10,577 per kg to ₹1,78,900.
  • The Fed kept its funds rate at 3.75%-4.00% on October 28, with two members favoring a 25-basis-point hike.
  • Global physically backed gold ETFs drew $31 billion in the third quarter of 2026, a quarterly record, per the World Gold Council.
  • Nepal's gold price fell ₹8,300 per tola in one day to ₹272,000, and India's festive and wedding demand is expected to support prices in November.

Sources: kitco.com, livemint.com, livemint.com, finance.biggo.com

Outlook: Bearish

Gold's plunge to a one-year low is driven by hawkish Fed expectations, rising oil and inflation worries, and a broad market sell-off. Record ETF inflows and upcoming Indian festive demand offer some cushion, but the near-term pressure dominates.
