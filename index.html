31 Oct 2026

  • The Court pressed the Reserve Bank of India to finalise its KYC Amendment Directions, 2026, requiring banks to flag suspected mule accounts and place temporary debit holds.
  • The Attorney-General assured the directions will be in force by December 31, and the Court insisted banks compensate victims, since the RBI's 2017 zero-liability circular covers only unauthorised transactions.

  • The editorial argues the scams thrive because victims fear high-handed police, and that India must pressure Myanmar, Cambodia and Laos, where trafficked Indians run scam compounds with some official patronage.
