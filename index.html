What the company does
  • Gabriel India, the ANAND Group flagship, makes ride-control products such as shock absorbers, struts and front forks, plus sunroofs, drivetrain parts and lubricants, across 500-plus models.
  • It sells to OEMs, the aftermarket and export customers, and reported ₹1,608 crore sales in the September 2026 quarter, with a market cap of ₹29,755 crore.
Why it moved today
  • Gabriel India fell 5.39% to ₹1,552.70 on 13 November 2026, a day after it hit a 20% upper circuit and a record ₹1,690.25 on the BSE, so the drop looks like profit-taking after that spike.
  • The 12 November surge came on a 20-fold jump in volume and a Choice Institutional Equities upgrade to 'ADD' on the Forvia seating joint venture, HL Mando Anand and HL Klemove platforms.
The numbers (Screener)
  • September 2026 quarter: sales ₹1,608 crore, up 22.9% year on year, operating profit ₹141 crore at a 9% margin, and net profit ₹124 crore.
  • The stock trades at a P/E of 71.8 and 13.2 times book value of ₹118, with ROCE of 25.6% and ROE of 20.2%.
What the numbers say
  • Gabriel's 25.6% ROCE and 20.2% ROE beat most peers, including Samvardhana Motherson at 13.4% and Bharat Forge at 12.6%, though Schaeffler India earns 27.9%.
  • Its P/E of 71.8 sits above the peer median near 60 and near Sona BLW's 71.2, so the 13 November fall only partly unwound the prior session's 16.5% gain.
